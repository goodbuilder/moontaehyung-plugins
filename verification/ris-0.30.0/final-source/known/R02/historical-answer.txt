A longer catalog with comparable detection completeness would be needed to infer which hemisphere has a higher underlying earthquake rate.

At M4+, the medians were 37 and 39. Label this measure *stations used per event*. A station can contribute to multiple earthquakes, and the packet supplies no station inventory, station coordinates, or uptime. It therefore cannot establish which hemisphere has better geographic coverage.
